# Employee Satisfaction & Workplace Improvement Analysis
**(REAL TIME DATA PROJECT)**

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an **Employee Satisfaction and Workplace Improvement Analysis** based on survey responses collected from **50 respondents**.
The analysis uses Microsoft Excel to organize questionnaire responses, calculate percentages, and understand employee opinions regarding workplace satisfaction, engagement, recognition, improvement initiatives, and areas affecting productivity.
The project contains **22 questionnaire-based analyses** along with a dashboard section.
---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze employee demographics and background information.
* Measure employee satisfaction using survey responses.
* Understand employee opinions about the workplace environment.
* Identify factors that influence employee motivation and satisfaction.
* Analyze employee participation in improvement activities.
* Identify workplace challenges affecting productivity.
* Understand employee preferences regarding recognition and rewards.
* Identify areas where management can improve employee engagement.
* Provide data-driven insights for workplace improvement.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Some important observations from the analysis include:

### 👥 Demographics

* The survey includes **50 respondents**.
* The majority of respondents are male.
* Respondents belong to different age groups and experience levels.
* All respondents in the dataset are classified as **permanent employees**.

### ⭐ Employee Satisfaction

The Likert-scale questions show a generally positive response pattern, with a large proportion of respondents selecting **Agree** or **Strongly Agree** for several workplace-related statements.

### 🏆 Employee Satisfaction Factors

The major factors identified include:

* Supportive work environment and teamwork
* Training and development opportunities
* Recognition and rewards
* Career growth and promotion opportunities

Among these factors, **supportive work environment and teamwork** and **training and development opportunities** received significant responses.

### 💡 Employee Improvement Participation

Employees identified several ways to improve workplace participation, including:

* Active participation in DSI (Daily Small Improvement)
* Regular employee satisfaction surveys
* Encouraging employees to share process-improvement ideas
* Effective implementation of 5S

### ⚠️ Workplace Challenges

The analysis identifies several factors that can affect workplace productivity:

* Personal mobile phone usage during working hours
* Stress and fatigue
* Time wasted searching for tools
* Absenteeism

### 🎖️ Recognition & Rewards

Employees showed interest in different recognition methods, particularly:

* Promotion and career advancement
* Appreciation from supervisors
* Employee of the Month awards
* Salary increments

### 🚀 Suggested Improvements

The survey highlights the following improvement areas:

* Strengthening the existing DSI program
* Reducing idle time during production
* Increasing employee participation in decision-making
* Conducting employee feedback and suggestion programs

## 📈 Methodology

The project follows these basic steps:

1. Collect employee survey responses.
2. Categorize responses according to each questionnaire.
3. Calculate the number of respondents for each option.
4. Calculate percentages using Excel formulas.
5. Analyze response patterns.
6. Identify major employee satisfaction factors.
7. Identify workplace challenges.
8. Identify employee suggestions for improvement.
9. Summarize the findings for management decision-making.

---

## 🧮 Data Analysis

Percentages were calculated using the number of responses for each option divided by the total number of respondents.

For example:

```excel
=Number of Respondents / Total Respondents
```

With 50 respondents:

```excel
=D3/50
```

This approach was used throughout the questionnaire analysis.
